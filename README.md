# Hourglass

**Hourglass:Time Bank – Where Time Becomes the Currency**

Imagine a world where **time, not money, is the currency**. A Time Bank is a community platform where every hour of service is worth one **Time Credit**, regardless of who provides it. A college student can help an elderly person with groceries for an hour and earn one credit. Later, they can use that credit to learn guitar, get career guidance, or receive help with any other skill from someone else. No money is exchanged—only time.

The goal is to build stronger communities by connecting people who need help with those who have the time and skills to offer it. It addresses real social issues such as elderly isolation, lack of access to affordable services, unequal learning opportunities, and weak neighborhood connections. It promotes volunteering, skill sharing, and mutual support while ensuring everyone's contribution is valued equally.

The website itself would be a highly interactive, storytelling-driven experience rather than a traditional platform. Through cinematic scrolling, immersive animations, interactive maps, and beautiful visualizations, users discover how one hour of kindness can create a chain of positive impact. The platform would include a Time Wallet to track earned credits, a marketplace of skills and services, community stories, achievement badges, and an impact dashboard showing hours shared and lives improved.

This concept aligns with several UN Sustainable Development Goals, including **Good Health & Well-being (SDG 3)**, **Quality Education (SDG 4)**, **Decent Work & Economic Growth (SDG 8)**, **Reduced Inequalities (SDG 10)**, and **Sustainable Cities & Communities (SDG 11)**. More than just a website, Time Bank reimagines how communities can support one another by proving that everyone's time has equal value.
