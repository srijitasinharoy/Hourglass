How it Works

The dial only moves one way — forward, back to you.

Four moments, arranged like quarter-hours, that make up every exchange. Click a point on the dial or a step below.

:00

You help someone

Fix a laptop, walk a dog, teach a chord progression — anything you can do in an hour, for anyone nearby who needs it.

:15

You earn a time credit

One hour given equals one credit, whether you're a surgeon or a sixteen-year-old who's good with Excel.

:30

You spend it on anything

Guitar lessons, French practice, someone to help you move a couch — spend your hour wherever you need it.

:45

Someone helps you back

The hour you gave away comes back around, just from a different direction and a different person.
